5. Minimum Sizes & Usage Rules

Minimum Size Requirements:

  • Print: Minimum width 20mm for stationery
  • Clear Space: Minimum margin equal to diamond facet height (X)
Mandatory Rules:
  • Always use official high-resolution PNG/WebP files
  • Do not alter aspect ratio or colors
  • Ensure high contrast against dark and light canvases
