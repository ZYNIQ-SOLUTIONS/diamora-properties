1. Statutory Regulatory Framework

As a licensed real estate advisory firm operating as a Designated Non-Financial Business and Profession (DNFBP) in the United Arab Emirates, Diamora Properties LLC is subject to the stringent oversight of the UAE Ministry of Economy (MoE) and the Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation (EOCN).

We operate in full compliance with:

  • Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Financing of Illegal Organisations (AML/CFT).
  • Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019 concerning the implementing regulation of Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018.
  • Cabinet Resolution No. (58) of 2020 regulating the Beneficial Ownership Procedures.
  • Financial Action Task Force (FATF) global recommendations for high-value real estate asset conveyance.
DNFBP Statutory Compliance Mandate

Every transaction brokered by Diamora Properties is subjected to automated multi-jurisdictional sanctions screening, source of funds validation, and regulatory verification prior to executing any binding property reservation or escrow deposit.

2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Protocols

Prior to initiating transaction documentation, property reservations, or private off-market viewings, our compliance division performs standard Customer Due Diligence (CDD):

3. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Where high-risk indicators are present—such as cross-border transactions involving high-risk jurisdictions identified by the FATF, high-value cash settlement proposals, or multi-layered offshore ownership vehicles—Diamora Properties implements Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).

EDD requires independent auditing of the buyer's Source of Wealth (SoW) and Source of Funds (SoF) through verified banking statements, audited corporate financials, audited dividends, or inheritance probate records before funds may enter master developer escrow accounts.

4. Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Transparency

In accordance with Cabinet Resolution No. (58) of 2020, Diamora Properties maintains an unwavering commitment to corporate transparency:

  • We require the identification and verification of any natural person who ultimately owns or controls 25% or more of the voting shares or capital in an acquiring corporate entity.
  • In the absence of a 25% owner, the natural person holding the position of senior managing official is identified and recorded in compliance dossiers.
  • Transactions utilizing anonymous bearer shares or non-transparent nominee arrangements are strictly declined.

5. goAML & Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) Reporting

Diamora Properties is fully integrated with the goAML portal administered by the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU):

  • Real Estate Activity Reports (REAR): All real estate purchases, sales, or leases involving cash payments equal to or exceeding AED 55,000 (whether in a single transaction or multiple linked operations) are filed via REAR to the FIU.
  • Virtual Asset Transfers (VASP): Transactions involving virtual assets or cryptocurrencies are strictly monitored and reported in alignment with UAE Central Bank and VARA guidelines.
  • Suspicious Transaction Reports (STR / SAR): Any transaction exhibiting patterns inconsistent with known commercial profiles or suggestive of illicit origin is reported immediately to the FIU under statutory whistleblower protection.

6. Regulated Settlement & Escrow Frameworks

To maintain the highest level of fiduciary security:

  • All purchase deposits and milestone installment payments are conducted through Official Developer Project Escrow Accounts regulated by the Abu Dhabi Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT) or Dubai Land Department (DLD).
  • Diamora Properties does not accept physical cash settlements for sovereign property conveyancing exceeding regulatory statutory limits.
  • Wire transfers must originate exclusively from verified accounts held in the legal name of the verified contracting buyer.

7. Sanctions & Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)

We conduct automated real-time screening of all clients, beneficial owners, and authorized signatories against:

  • The UAE Local Terrorist List issued by the UAE Supreme Council for National Security.
  • United Nations Security Council Consolidated Sanctions Lists (UNSC).
  • OFAC (US), EU, and UK HM Treasury international financial sanctions registers.
  • Global Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) databases, including family members and close associates (RCAs).
