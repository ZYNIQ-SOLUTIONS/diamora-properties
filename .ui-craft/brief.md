# Diamora Properties — Product & Design Brief

## 1. Product Identity
- **Brand**: Diamora Properties
- **Sector**: Ultra-Luxury Real Estate & Prime Investment Advisory (Dubai & Abu Dhabi, UAE)
- **Visual Personality**: High-end architectural luxury, precision geometry, Imperial Gold on Diamond Alabaster light theme.

## 2. Target Audience
- High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWIs), global real estate investors, and luxury homeowners seeking exclusive UAE properties, off-market allocations, and UAE Golden Visa advisory.

## 3. Brand Assets & Usage Rules
- **Light Mode Default**: All web surfaces are rendered in light mode (`#FDFBF7` canvas, `#FAF6EC` surfaces).
- **Logo Placement**:
  - Light Backgrounds / Navigation / Footer: `assets/logos/logo-gold.png` (transparent background with gold emblem/text and dark subtitle) or signature vector SVG marks.
  - Interactive Preloader: Animated Imperial Gold Emblem with tracking typography reveal.
  - Favicon: `assets/logos/logo-preview.png` and `assets/logos/logo-square.png`.

## 4. Key Motion Guidelines
- **Preloader Animation**: Smooth light-mode curtain reveal with animated drawing of the Diamora gold diamond emblem, counter progression, and fluid entrance transition.
- **Scroll Effects**: GSAP ScrollTrigger staggered reveals on cards, subtle gold line drawings, and micro-interactions.
