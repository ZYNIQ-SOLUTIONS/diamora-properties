2. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small encrypted alphanumeric data files placed on your device by websites you visit. Similar technologies include HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage, which allow modern web applications to store preferences without transmitting personal identifiers across networks.

Diamora Properties adheres to the principle of Data Minimization: we do not utilize cross-site tracking beacons, programmatic data brokerage pixels, or invasive profiling algorithms.

4. Browser-Level Cookie Management

You may also restrict, block, or purge cookies directly through your web browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact map responsiveness and authentication:

  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Block All Cookies.
  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-Party Cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.
