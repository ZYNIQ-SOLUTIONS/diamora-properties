1. Executive Overview & Data Controller

Diamora Properties LLC ("Diamora", "we", "our", or "us"), registered under P.O. Box 92813, United Arab Emirates, acts as the Data Controller responsible for the processing and protection of personal data collected through this website, private investor portals, and direct advisory consultations.

As an elite brokerage and advisory firm handling sovereign acquisitions, off-market penthouses, and private estates across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Ras Al Khaimah, we hold discretion and data security as foundational pillars of our client relationships. This policy outlines how your personal information is gathered, safeguarded, and processed in strict alignment with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 regarding the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) and global benchmarks including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Statutory UAE Data Protection Compliance

All electronic communications, passport verifications, and property portfolio dossiers are stored within high-security data centers with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is strictly necessary to fulfill high-tier real estate advisory, sovereign property transactions, and regulatory know-your-customer (KYC) requirements:

  • Contact Identification: Full legal name, title, primary residence country, verified email address, telephone and encrypted WhatsApp contact credentials.
  • Acquisition Preferences: Investment budgets, desired asset classes (waterfront villas, private islands, penthouses, commercial assets), geographical focus (Saadiyat Cultural District, Al Bateen, Palm Jumeirah), and financing requirements.
  • Statutory KYC & Verification Data: Government-issued passport copies, Emirates ID, proof of address, source of wealth certifications, and Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) disclosures required by the UAE Ministry of Economy and Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).
  • Digital Telemetry: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, session telemetry, and interaction metrics gathered solely for performance optimization and cybersecurity defense.

4. Sovereign & High-Net-Worth Confidentiality

Diamora Properties operates a specialized Non-Disclosure Protocol for sovereign entities, royal houses, family offices, and institutional investors.

Client identities associated with off-market trophy assets (including private beachfront estates and penthouses) are pseudonymized across internal deal desks. Physical documents and digital KYC dossiers are sequestered within role-gated vaults accessible only by licensed compliance officers and the managing advisory partner.

5. Authorized Third-Party Disclosures

We never sell, lease, or commercially monetize client records. Personal information is only disclosed to authorized institutional counterparties strictly required to complete property conveyancing:

  • Government Regulatory Registries: Abu Dhabi Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT / DARI), Dubai Land Department (DLD / RERA), and municipal notary bodies for official title deed issuance.
  • Master Developers: Aldar Properties, Emaar, Meraas, Modon, Nakheel, and sovereign master developers for off-plan allocations and NOC releases.
  • Licensed Escrow & Banking Partners: UAE Central Bank-regulated financial institutions for certified escrow management and mortgage settlements.
  • Statutory Authorities: UAE Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) or judicial law enforcement solely upon receiving authenticated sovereign subpoenas.

6. Technical & Organizational Security Standards

We implement enterprise-grade security controls to safeguard data integrity and prevent unauthorized breach:

  • End-to-end cryptographic hashing and TLS 1.3 encryption across all public and admin endpoints.
  • Zero-trust administrative access enforced through hardware Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Automated real-time anomaly detection, rate limiting, and intrusion prevention defense protocols.
  • Continuous third-party security audits and annual ISO/IEC 27001 vulnerability assessments.

7. Your Statutory Rights

Under the UAE Data Protection Law, verified clients and users retain the following statutory entitlements:

  • Right of Access: Request a comprehensive export of all personal data held under your name or entity.
  • Right to Rectification: Require instant correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request full deletion of marketing telemetry and non-mandatory historical records (subject to statutory 5-year AML retention rules).
  • Right to Restriction: Temporarily suspend processing of specific datasets during ongoing disputes.
  • Right to Revoke Consent: Withdraw consent for marketing briefings and newsletters at any moment via one-click unsubscribe or direct notice.
